🧭 Getting Started

1. Log in and verify session
Use the dashboard verify session button to confirm your token is active on the correct backend.
2. Create a short link
Paste your URL, optionally add a custom slug, set expiry, then choose Backend2 or AWS.
3. Upgrade if needed
If Backend2 slot limits are reached, open upgrade.html and create a payment request.
4. Track analytics
Use analytics.html to monitor clicks, IP activity, risk score, and geo map traffic.
5. Manage extras
Landing pages and domains are handled separately in landing.html and domain.html.

🛠 Common Fixes

Session expired
Log in again. If the token is missing or invalid, the dashboard cannot load protected data.
Payment still pending
Wait for admin confirmation after submitting transfer. Keep your payment ID ready.
Backend2 creation blocked
Your plan may be paused, expired, or full on active short-link slots.
Domain verification failed
Check the TXT record value carefully and make sure proxy settings do not affect ownership verification flow.
No analytics shown
The link may be live but may not have any recorded clicks yet.
