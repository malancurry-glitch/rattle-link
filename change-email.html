✉️ Account email security

Change your login email safely without breaking your account access.

This page lets you update the email used for login, verification, reset, and secure account recovery. It supports both the normal confirmation flow and a logged-in lost-mailbox recovery flow.

Standard email change Enter your new email, current password, and 2FA code if enabled. The new inbox receives a confirmation link before the account is updated.
Lost access to old mailbox If you are already logged in but cannot access your old email anymore, recover the account by confirming your password and 2FA, then switch to the new email directly.
Safe by default No public email reveal flow is exposed. This page only works for logged-in users with a valid session token.
